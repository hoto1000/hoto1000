# Hi, I'm Hoto 🪐

I build small, free, plain-language resources for people who are having a hard time, in as many languages as I can manage.

## Projects

| | |
|---|---|
| **[Planet Hoto](https://github.com/hoto1000/planet-hoto)** · [hoto.lol](https://hoto.lol) | Mental health help for the practical side of life: finding care, paying for it, keeping a job and a home, and asking for help. Seven languages. No accounts, no tracking. |
| **[Hotolosophy](https://github.com/hoto1000/hotolosophy)** · [hotolosophy.com](https://hotolosophy.com) | A resource kit for people and AI agents working against trafficking, sexual exploitation, and war, using lawful, non-harmful actions. |

## How you can help

- **Translate** a guide or fix a translation. Native speakers make the biggest difference.
- **Add or correct crisis lines** and local services for your country.
- **Make a guide clearer or shorter.** If a paragraph confused you, that is a bug.
- **Improve accessibility**: screen readers, contrast, keyboard use.

Planet Hoto has a contributor guide and issues labeled `good first issue`. Hotolosophy has rules for AI agents in AGENTS.md. A ⭐ on a repo helps other people find it.

## Principles

Plain words over jargon. Private by default. Free forever. Equity first: care is harder to reach for some people, and the guides say so.
